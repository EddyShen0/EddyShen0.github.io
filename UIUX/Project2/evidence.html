Source library

Real publications · Checked November 23, 2026

The discussion uses historical 2023–2024 data and simulated participants. Some example claims intentionally misinterpret those data. Read the original sources below; these are not current-year estimates or automated fact checks.

Multiple links can refer to the same underlying dataset. Census companion publications and the CRS summary of HUD data do not represent independent measurements.

U.S. Census Bureau · Renter housing costs, 2023

September 12, 2024 · 2023 ACS estimates

Reports 49.7% among renter households with a calculated housing-cost burden. The denominator matters: this is not a share of all Americans.

https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024/renter-households-cost-burdened-race.html ↗

U.S. Census Bureau · Rent and utilities in 2023

September 12, 2024 · 2023 ACS analysis

Describes a 3.8% rise in inflation-adjusted median gross rental costs in 2023. This is an aggregate measure including utilities and fuels, not a uniform increase for every tenant.

https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2024/09/acs-rent-burden.html ↗

U.S. Census Bureau · 2023 ACS release bulletin

Official Census bulletin · Same ACS release, not independent evidence

Companion bulletin summarizing the same Census release. Used as corroborating context, not as a separate dataset.

https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USCENSUS/bulletins/3b55028 ↗

HUD · 2024 Homelessness Assessment, Part 1

December 2024 · January 2024 point-in-time estimate · PDF

The 2024 count totals 771,480 people, including 497,256 sheltered and 274,224 unsheltered. It estimates homelessness on a single night, not unique people over an entire year.

https://www.huduser.gov/portal/sites/default/files/pdf/2024-AHAR-Part-1.pdf ↗

Congressional Research Service · Homelessness (IF12985)

May 5, 2025 version reviewed · Explains the same HUD dataset

Explains the distinction between point-in-time counts and annual estimates. Its 2024 figures derive from HUD, so these two sources are not independent counts.

https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF12985 ↗
